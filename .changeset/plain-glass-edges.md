Plainer edges. Glass surfaces, fields and menus lose the two-tone pink / blue rim for a plain white border with a thin highlight along the top (`--rim-edge`, new `--edge-highlight` token). The `solid` and `outline` looks drop their animated holographic border: `solid` is a flat fill, `outline` a 1.5 px border in the tone's text colour. The holographic film stays on the `holo` look, the `strong` card, the switch, the progress bar and the dialog header.
