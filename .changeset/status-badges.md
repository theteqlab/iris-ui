Status badges: `<Badge status="success">` (and `warning`, `danger`, `info`, `neutral`) shows a state in the status colours of Alert and Toast, with a dot. Badges are now flat (no drop shadow), slightly narrower and semibold. Solid and tinted holo buttons get a tighter shadow and lift by a pixel on hover. `--info` is now the same blue in every palette (it was the palette's accent, coral in Couchant), so that it never reads as an error.
