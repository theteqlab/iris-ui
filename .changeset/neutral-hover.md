Neutral hover. The highlighted option of menus and lists, and the hover of ghost buttons, tabs, toggles, accordion headers, the password button and the toast buttons, now share one neutral tint of the text colour (new `--hover-bg` token; `--menu-hover` points to it). It replaces the holographic gradient of light mode and the accent tint of dark mode.
