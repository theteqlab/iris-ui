Add `Flag`: a country's flag as an SVG from flagcdn.com (or your own `baseUrl`), in three shapes and sizes, named after the country in the language of `LocaleProvider` through `Intl.DisplayNames`, with the country code as a fallback. `Messages` gains an optional `locale`; new `--radius-flag` token.
