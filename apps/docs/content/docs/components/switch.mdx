## Features

- A label is required by the types: visible children, `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- Three sizes; the thumb slides in 200 ms (no animation with reduced motion).
- Controlled or uncontrolled; submits with a form.
- Built on [Radix Switch](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/switch).

## Accessibility

Follows the [WAI-ARIA switch pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/switch/):
`role="switch"` with `aria-checked`. Use a switch for a setting that applies at once,
and a [Checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox) for a choice submitted with a form.
